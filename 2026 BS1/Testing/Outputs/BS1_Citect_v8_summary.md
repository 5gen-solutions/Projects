## Mapping rules learned (from his 1,462 References rows vs the "Tags needed for Runtime Params" tab)
- One reference per `Tile_Interlock` / `Tile_Interlocking` line that has `Interlock:` or `Not_Interlock:`.
- **Equipment** = the tab row's Equipment (col A), i.e. the equipment whose status page shows the interlock.
- **Referenced Item**: `Not_Interlock:X` → `NOT_X`; `Interlock:X` → `X`. On permissive lines (`Tile_Interlocking`): `Col1:Inv` → `X`; `Col1:Nor` → `NOT_X` when a NOT_X tag exists, otherwise `X`. Spelled with the SCADA Tags capitalisation.
- **Referenced Equipment** = SCADA Tags Equipment of the interlock tag (= the alarm's Equipment). If the tag isn't in SCADA Tags, the SCADA Equip whose Tag Prefix matches; otherwise left blank.
- **Category** comes from the section heading, not from the alarm Cat: "Trips", "Drive A/B/C Trips", "All of the following" and headerless continuation pages (Status 2/3/4) → IL_Critical; "Auto Trips" → IL_NonCritical; "[Maintenance ][Extend|Retract|Forward|Reverse ]Start/Run[ Forward|Reverse] Permissives" → IL_[Local|Seq][Fwd|Rev][Start|Run] (Extend/Forward=Fwd, Retract/Reverse=Rev, Maintenance=Local).
- **Order** = position within Equipment+Category in tab order, counting across sections and pages and counting the XXX placeholders (which get no row).
- Cluster and Referenced Cluster = BR4_Cluster1; Project = BS1_Tags; Association, Comment and Custom 1–8 blank; "Found in" columns only filled on row 2.
- Skipped like Tim: "None" self-reference lines and XXX placeholders.
